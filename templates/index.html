About SkillSwap

SkillSwap is an online platform where people can exchange their knowledge and skills. One person can teach a skill and learn another skill from someone else. Our main goal is to encourage peer-to-peer learning and help everyone improve their abilities.

How SkillSwap Works

1. Register

Create your free SkillSwap account.

2. Add Skills

Select the skills you can teach and learn.

3. Find People

Connect with people who share your interests.

4. Exchange Skills

Teach others and learn something new.
